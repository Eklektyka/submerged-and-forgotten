# Submerged and Forgotten — closed 2026-05

**Question:** Among resolved missing persons on charleyproject.org, what
fraction involve a vehicle found crashed or submerged — and when a person
disappears with their own vehicle, what is the probability it ends up in
water?

**What I expected:** Not recorded. No pre-registration.

**What I found:**

- 148/1,995 resolved cases (7.4%) involved a vehicle found crashed
  (`results/summary.txt`)
- 127/148 (85.8%) of crashed vehicles were found in water
  (`results/crashed_vehicle_summary.txt`)
- 249 cases had a vehicle associated with the disappearance; 190 involved
  the person's own vehicle (`results/own_vehicle_hypothesis.txt`)
- P(vehicle found crashed | own vehicle missing) = 69.5% (132/190)
  (`results/own_vehicle_hypothesis.txt`)
- Median time to discovery for water cases: 2,424 days (~6.6 years)
  (`results/location_summary.txt`)
- 57.4% of crashed-vehicle cases: person's remains found inside the car
  when recovered (`results/discovery_order_summary.txt`)

**Why I stopped:** The descriptive analysis is complete. All resolved cases
on the site were scraped and classified. No further data collection is needed.

**What it rules out:** Nothing — this is descriptive, not causal.

**What would reopen it:** A partnership with a dive team or law enforcement
to apply these base rates to active cases. Findings were shared with
Adventures With Purpose. Case-level data is not published (S2).
