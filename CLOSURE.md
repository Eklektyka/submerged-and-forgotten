# Submerged and Forgotten — closed 2026-05

**Question:** Among resolved missing persons on charleyproject.org, what
fraction involve a vehicle found crashed or submerged?

**What I expected:** Not recorded. No hypothesis, no pre-registration, no
research plan. This was an exploratory data-collection exercise.

**What I found:** 1,995 resolved Charley Project cases scraped alphabetically
(A–Z) and classified by claude-haiku for vehicle-crash involvement.
Classification fields: vehicle_found_crashed (boolean), confidence
(high/medium/low), evidence (one quoted sentence).
(`charley_results.jsonl`)

No aggregate statistics were computed. No cross-tabulation by classification
category or geography was performed.

**Why I stopped:** The scraper completed its pass; no downstream analysis was
ever defined or started. The project was a self-contained data-collection
exercise.

**What it rules out:** Nothing — no analysis was performed.

**What would reopen it:** A defined research question and an IRB or ethics
review, given that the data contains names and narratives of missing persons.
Case-level data is not published (S2).
